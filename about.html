MILAN DEKKERS

I’m Milan, the photographer behind Trailbrake Visuals.

I’ve always been drawn to motorsport, not just the cars, but everything that comes with them. The noise, the atmosphere, the people, the pressure and those split seconds that can make a race unforgettable.

Most of my experience behind the camera has been built at the Nürburgring, where I’ve spent countless hours learning to anticipate the moments worth capturing.

For me, photography is about being there when something happens and having the instinct to capture it. Turning a fleeting moment on track into something that lasts.

I’ll be taking that experience with me to new circuits and new races as Trailbrake Visuals continues to grow.

01

Anticipate.

Motorsport moves quickly. The best frames often come from knowing what is about to happen before it happens.

02

React.

Racing is unpredictable. Being ready to react to those unexpected moments is what makes motorsport photography exciting.

03

Remember.

A photograph should do more than show a car. It should bring back the feeling of being there.
